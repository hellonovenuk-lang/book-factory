# Book ideas

Ideas for the next books, in the same shape as the Golf, Padel and Runner's
books: a straight-faced official programme or self-help manual for a UK hobby
obsessive, bought by the people who live with him (or her) and handed over at
Christmas, a birthday or Father's Day. Black and white, 80 pages, one named
main character, big-lettering cover.

The title pattern is **"The ___ Addict's Guide to [the normal thing they've
stopped doing]"**. Each idea lists who buys it and three "that's literally
him" behaviours to start the brief with.

Drafted by Claude 2026-09-27. Nothing here is a book yet: start one with
`/write-book` and the title.

## Top picks

1. **The Cyclist's Guide to Leaving the House Without Lycra**
   Bought by: his wife, who has been told the new bike "was about £800".
   - Plans family days out around a café 40 miles away he'll ride to.
   - Won't count a ride unless it's on Strava; re-rides it if the GPS dropped.
   - Shaves his legs "for aerodynamics" and "in case of a crash".

2. **The Hyrox Addict's Guide to Talking About Anything Else**
   Bought by: a partner, a colleague, the group chat. Works for him or her.
   - Knows their wall-ball split to the second; not their child's shoe size.
   - Turns every garden, beach and car park into a sled push.
   - Has booked the next race abroad and calls it "a holiday".

3. **The Barbecue Dad's Guide to Cooking Indoors**
   Bought by: his wife, whose kitchen now only makes side salads.
   - Got up at 4am to start a brisket for a lunch at 1.
   - Owns three thermometers, a smoker and an opinion about charcoal.
   - Barbecues in November, in a coat, under an umbrella.

## More ideas

4. **The Fishing Addict's Guide to Coming Home Before Dark**: a 48-hour carp
   session "just the one night"; a bivvy that cost more than the car.
5. **The Fantasy Football Manager's Guide to Real Life**: goes silent at
   Saturday dinner over a captaincy pick; the transfer deadline is in the
   family calendar, the dentist is not.
6. **The Wild Swimmer's Guide to Warm Water**: the cold plunge "for
   inflammation"; tells strangers the water temperature unasked.
7. **The Gym Addict's Guide to Eating a Normal Dinner**: weighs the
   chicken; brings a shaker to a wedding.
8. **The F1 Fan's Guide to Sunday Lunch**: the roast is timed around lights
   out; knows every team principal, not his neighbours.
9. **The Season Ticket Holder's Guide to Free Weekends**: every family event
   checked against the fixture list; away days to Plymouth on a Tuesday.
10. **The Craft Beer Bore's Guide to Just Having a Pint**: can't order
    without asking what's on cask; Untappd before small talk.
11. **The Ski Bore's Guide to the Other 51 Weeks**: books next season on the
    drive home; still talks about the powder day in 2019.
12. **The Cricket Addict's Guide to the Summer Holidays**: a five-day Test
    is a reason, not a sport; the family holiday is planned round the
    Ashes.
13. **The Warhammer Addict's Guide to Unpainted Walls**: the spare room is
    an army; the house hasn't been decorated in six years.
14. **The Allotment Addict's Guide to Weekends Indoors**: brings home 40
    courgettes and a feud with plot 12.
15. **The Coffee Snob's Guide to Instant**: weighs the beans, travels with a
    grinder, can't drink a hotel coffee without a speech.

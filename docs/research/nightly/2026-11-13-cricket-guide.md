## 1. Decision needed

## 2. The pitch

- **Title (working):** *The Cricket Addict's Guide to the Summer Holidays: An Official Programme for the Family Who Would Like to Go Somewhere in August*
- **Who buys it:** his wife, partner or grown-up kids, for the man who plays for the Sunday thirds, scores the match in a book and follows the Test on his phone at a wedding.
- **When:** Father's Day, Sunday 20 June 2027. The first men's Ashes Test in England starts on Friday 18 June 2027, two days earlier, and the series runs to early August. Cricket will be on every front page that week.
- **Why now:** it is the only idea on our list with a fixed national event sitting on top of a gift date, and there are seven months to make it without a rush.
- **Different from Amazon:** every cricket humour book in the top 30 is for the cricketer and about cricket (quotes, anecdotes, commentary-box stories, phrase books). None is about living with him, and none is a manual the family reads aloud.

## 4. The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 in, black and white, 80 pages, the same as Golf and Padel. £9.99 UK / $9.99 US, about £4.06 a copy in the UK (royalty table in `docs/research/2026-09-30-new-book-types.md`). This is a UK, Australia and India book; expect little from the US.
- **Pen name:** the Dave brand, same as the live books.
- **Main character:** a new named man in the Dave world (the intake asks for his fixed details). Suggested: an opening bat for the Sunday thirds, average 11, who describes himself as "more of a nurdler".
- **Shape:** an official tour itinerary and playing conditions, issued by the family, for a summer in which he is also expected to be present.
- **Contents:**
  1. Front matter: notice of selection, playing conditions, how to read this programme aloud
  2. **Stage 1 The Fixture List.** How the family calendar came to have "Away, Little Snoring" on it and not the dentist. No. 01 diagnostic: tick the boxes that apply.
  3. **Stage 2 The Kit.** The bag in the hall since April, the bat that is being "knocked in" in the living room, whites in with the coloured wash.
  4. **Stage 3 The Test Match.** Five days is a reason, not a sport. Radio in the garden, phone under the table, the score given to people who did not ask.
  5. **Stage 4 The Tea.** His turn to do teas; what he believes a tea is; a formal table of what was sent and what came back.
  6. **Stage 5 The Holiday.** Planning two weeks abroad around a home Ashes; the villa must have a signal; a typeset gauge of how far the conversation is from cricket.
  7. **Stage 6 The Winter.** Indoor nets, the AGM, the tour to Australia "we could make a holiday of".
  8. Back matter: certificate of a completed summer, a cut-out card ("Bad light: I am stopping this conversation"), the family's scorecard.
- **Pictures:** chapter openers only (the default budget): 6 openers plus the cover, about 14 credits at 2 each, plus redraws. Every activity panel, table, gauge and card is typeset and costs nothing.
- **Cover direction:** big title lettering; one family scene in full colour, as on the new Golf and Padel covers: a family at a beach picnic, him in whites and pads holding a radio to his ear. Gift line: "The perfect gift for the cricketer who has everything except a free Saturday."

## 5. Samples (original, first drafts)

**Back cover:** "He said the season finishes in September. He did not say which one. This programme has been issued by the people who live with him, for a summer in which he is also expected to attend. It covers the fixture list, the kit bag in the hall, the five-day Test, the tea, and a holiday in August. Read it aloud. He will ask what the score is."

**Notice of selection (front matter).** You have been selected for the summer. The selectors are the people in your house. They met in the kitchen, without minutes, and the vote was unanimous. Selection does not mean anybody is cross with you. It means that a holiday has been booked, that it is in August, and that you are in the squad for all fourteen days, including the Thursday.

**Stage 1, The Fixture List.** A cricket season is issued in March as one sheet of paper. It goes on the fridge. By May it is the only document in the house that everybody consults. Your daughter's birthday is on it, in pencil, next to "Home, 1.30 start". Nobody remembers who wrote that. The handwriting is yours.

**No. 01 · Diagnostic: Is It Affecting the Household?** Tick each one that has happened since April. [ ] He has described rain as "frustrating" on a day with no match. [ ] He has given the score to a waiter. [ ] He has said "we" about eleven men he has never met. [ ] A bat has been oiled on a surface used for food. [ ] He has left a christening "to see how they're getting on". Score: 0-1, a normal summer. 2-3, the family should agree a signal. 4-5, check the fridge; you are probably on the fixture list.

**Stage 4, The Tea.** Every member takes a turn at teas. He volunteered in the bar, in February, when it was a long way off. He believes a tea is forty-four sandwiches and a cake. He does not know where the sandwiches come from. He has now asked, on a Friday night, whether we have "one of those big teapots".

(These show the voice only; the manuscript pass checks them against `style/voice-bible.md`, the banned phrases and content QA.)

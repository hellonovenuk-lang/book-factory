# Proposal: a funny large-print puzzle book for the newly retired (angle 2, a new format for the same buyers)

Nightly research run, 19 October 2026. The last run (16 October, Hyrox) was angle 1, so this is angle 2.

## 1. Decision needed

**Book: *The Newly Retired Man's Puzzle Book*, a large-print book of word searches, quizzes and "spot the excuse" puzzles in the Dave voice, sold next to the retirement guide Kieran already said go to. Reply `go` or `no` (and why).**

## 2. The pitch

- **Title (working):** *The Newly Retired Man's Puzzle Book: Large-Print Puzzles for the Man Who Now Has the Whole Day, and the Household That Would Like Some of It Back*
- **Who buys it:** the wife, the grown-up kids and the colleagues' whip-round, for a man who has just stopped work. A puzzle book is a gift he will actually use at 10am on a Tuesday, which a joke book is not.
- **When:** retirements happen all year, so it is not tied to one date. Best moments: January (many people retire at the tax year or new year), Father's Day (live by early May) and next Christmas. This Christmas is not realistic (see section 7).
- **Why now:** the retirement guide is already approved to be made (`go`, 2 October), so the puzzle book can be a sequel that sells beside it. It also shares the puzzle-page build with the murder-mystery proposal (14 October), so the build work pays twice.
- **Different from Amazon:** the retirement puzzle books I found are nostalgia-led (pop culture, history, food, travel) from a big publisher. None is written in the household's voice, about the actual change: him being home.

## 4. The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 in, black and white, about 100 pages, **large print** (big grids, type at least 14 pt). £8.99 UK / $9.99 US. KDP printing cost up to 110 pages is £1.93 / $2.30 (first report), so about **£3.46 a copy UK and $3.69 US**. Check KDP's own calculator at upload.
- **Pen name:** the Dave brand, same big-lettering cover as the other books, subtitle marked "Large Print".
- **Contents (about 100 pages):**
  1. Official notice and how to use this book ("not to be completed during the school run")
  2. Word searches, 14 puzzles with joke themes: "Things in the Kitchen He Has Reorganised", "Jobs That Were Quick", "Phrases From 1987", "Sheds"
  3. "Spot the Excuse": 10 short scenes; which of the five reasons is the real one for not going to the garden centre?
  4. The Retirement Pub Quiz: 10 quizzes of 10 questions (general knowledge with household twists), answers at the back
  5. Criss-cross (a word fit-in grid, no clue-writing needed) with joke word lists
  6. "Who Said It?": him, her or the dishwasher
  7. Logic puzzle: the Weekly Timetable (who is allowed in the kitchen, and when)
  8. The Project Register: a grid he fills in, with "estimated" and "actual" columns
  9. Solutions and a cut-out Certificate of Completion
- **Pictures:** none inside. One cover picture (about 2 credits). I could not read the Higgsfield balance (the connector is not available in this scheduled run); last known 6 credits, and the retirement guide's cover will draw some. If credits are short, reuse the guide's cover scene cropped, as was done for Golf.
- **Cover direction:** same retired man at the kitchen table with a pencil and a word-search page, wife in the doorway with a tea towel, an empty band across the top for the title; the same style as the guide so the two sit as a pair.

## 5. Samples (original)

**Back cover:** "He spent forty years being needed by a company. He would now like to be needed by a crossword. This book has 100 pages of puzzles, all in large print, so he can do them without his glasses and without asking where his glasses are."

**Word search theme "Things That Were Quick Jobs":** hidden words: SHELF, GUTTER, LOFT, FENCE, TAP, BLIND, DECKING, HEDGE, "TWENTY MINUTES" (grid hides the first nine; the tenth is the one you must not look for).

**Spot the Excuse (sample):** "Why can't he come to the garden centre? (a) the football is on (b) he's waiting for a delivery (c) it'll be busy (d) he said it's not a good time of year for plants (e) he looked at the weather. Real answer: all five, in that order, over four minutes."

**Quiz sample:**
1. Roughly how long is "just popping to B&Q"? (a) twenty minutes (b) the afternoon (c) until the tea is cold (d) there is no known answer
2. Which cupboard has he reorganised twice since March?
3. How many "quick jobs" are on the register at the start of week 3?

**Who Said It? (sample):** "I'm not getting in your way, I'm just standing here." Him / Her / The Dishwasher. (Him. It is always him.)

## 6. What Book Factory needs

- **Today:** page types for text, checklists, tables, quizzes (`activity` blocks) and certificates. The quiz, "Spot the excuse", "Who said it?", Project Register and certificate pages can be made now.
- **Build work first:** a **puzzle page** (word-search grid with answer key, set as real type) and a simple **criss-cross grid**. This is the same build as `IDEAS.md` 2026-09-30 and the murder-mystery proposal, so doing it once serves all three books. Estimate: **2 phases** (1: word-search generator, grid page and answer key with tests; 2: criss-cross and a large-print page template), one sitting each. Large-print needs a check that grid cells stay readable at 6 x 9.
- **Then:** about 3 sittings: the writing, the pictures (one cover) and the review. Puzzle correctness (every word really hidden, every answer matches) should be checked by the generator and a test, not by reading.
- Picture budget: `chapter_openers` is fine, since no page pictures are used.

## 8. Risks and runners-up

- **Risks:** thin sales evidence; the format needs a build before any copy; large-print crowds are a price fight on generic books (ours is a gift, not a generic puzzle book); puzzle errors would ruin reviews, so the generator must verify them; retiree might feel patronised, so keep the joke on the household too; KDP AI-disclosure box must be ticked.
- **Runner-up 1:** *The Hobby Addict's Quiz Night* (a pub-quiz book in the Dave voice for golf, padel and running fans); same build, wider buyer, no extra pictures.
- **Runner-up 2:** a "for her" twin of the retirement guide, *The Newly Retired Woman's Guide to Being Left Alone*; no build, but waits on the guide's results.

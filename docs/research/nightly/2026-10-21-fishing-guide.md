# The Fishing Addict's Guide to Coming Home Before Dark (Dave series)

## Decision needed

**Book: *The Fishing Addict's Guide to Coming Home Before Dark*. Reply `go` or `no` (and why).**

## The pitch

- **Title:** *The Fishing Addict's Guide to Coming Home Before Dark: An Official Handbook for the Angler, and the Household Left Holding the Dinner*
- **Who buys:** the wife, partner or grown-up child of a man who fishes (carp, coarse, sea or fly). Fishing gear is already a standard gift, so a book that jokes about the gear is an easy add-on.
- **When:** Christmas (live about mid-November), then Father's Day (June) and the new season in spring. Fishing is less seasonal than Father's Day books, since anglers fish all year.
- **Why now:** it is the runner-up in two earlier proposals, has the only direct sales signal I could find (below), and needs no build work. Earlier runs gave the barbecue book to Father's Day instead, so this is the better Christmas pick.
- **Different from Amazon:** the fishing humour I found is written for the angler (facts, tips, "tall tales"). None is written for the person at home, which is the Dave angle.

## The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 in, black and white, about 80 pages, £9.99 / $9.99, earning about £4.06 / $3.69 a copy (royalty table in `docs/research/2026-09-30-new-book-types.md`; check KDP's calculator before pricing). Dave brand (Kieran Smith), big-lettering cover like Golf and Padel.
- **Contents (section by section):**
  1. Official notice, and the Terms of Angling (a mock contract: "just the one night")
  2. Assessment: "Are you a fisherman or a man with a very expensive hobby?" (tick-list)
  3. The Session Length Converter: "a couple of hours" = what it means (table)
  4. The Gear Ledger: bivvy, rods, "the reel", set against the family car (write-in table)
  5. The Catch Register: the fish he caught (weight grows with every telling) against the fish he photographed
  6. The Excuses Index: tide, wind, pressure, "they weren't feeding"
  7. Weather Report Translator: what he checks at 4am
  8. Family Event Timetable: the wedding that clashes with the match
  9. The Return Home Gauge: how close to dark, how close to dinner
  10. The Smell Protocol: where the bag may and may not go
  11. Photo Etiquette: the grip-and-grin, with held-out-arm perspective
  12. Cut-out Certificate: Permission to Be Home for Tea
  13. The signed Household Treaty
- **Pictures:** one cover picture only (about 2 credits). I could not read the Higgsfield balance (not connected). Last known: 6 credits, before the Retirement cover is drawn. No interior pictures.
- **Cover direction:** Dave on the riverbank in a bivvy chair at dusk, rod bent, the family at the table behind him with a cold dinner, an empty band across the top for the title.

## The pitch

- **Title:** *The Cyclist's Guide to Leaving the House Without Lycra: An Official Handbook for the Man Who Rides, and the Household That Waits*
- **Who buys:** the wife or partner (and grown-up kids) of a man who cycles seriously: the bike that "was about £800", the 40-mile ride to a café, Strava as proof of life.
- **When:** Christmas first (live by about 7 November), then Father's Day (June) and birthdays. Cycling is a spring-to-autumn hobby, so it gets a second spike in spring.
- **Why now:** it is the top pick in `docs/BOOK-IDEAS.md`, needs no build work, and uses the exact Dave formula, so the three Dave books on the shelf sell it and it sells them.
- **Different from Amazon:** the cycling humour I found is cartoon collections, bluffer's guides and "look pro" tips, all written for the cyclist. None I found is a straight-faced official manual aimed at the household.

## The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 in, black and white, about 80 pages, £9.99 / $9.99, earning about £4.06 / $3.69 a copy. Dave brand, big-lettering cover like Golf and Padel.
- **Contents:**
  1. Official notice and Terms of Cycling (a mock contract)
  2. Assessment: "Are you a cyclist or a person who owns a bike?" (tick-list)
  3. The Cost Register: every part, what he said it cost, what it cost
  4. The Ride Distance Converter ("about an hour" to real hours; "nearly there" to miles)
  5. Strava Admission Rules: a ride that was not uploaded did not happen, and the re-ride
  6. The Café Stop Doctrine: a day out planned round a coffee 40 miles away
  7. Weekend Forecast: weather he will ride in, and the weather he will not
  8. The Leg Shaving Explanations (a numbered list of reasons)
  9. Kit Rota: where the lycra is drying (the radiator, the banister, the shower)
  10. Household Rules for Bike Storage (the hall, the bedroom, "the spare room")
  11. A Gauge: how close to home is "nearly home"
  12. Cut-out Certificate: Permission to Wear Normal Trousers
  13. The signed Household Treaty
- **Pictures:** one cover picture only (about 2 credits); I could not read the Higgsfield balance tonight (not connected here). Last known: 6 credits, minus the Retirement cover when drawn. Interior pictures: none.
- **Cover direction:** the family at the Sunday table, Dave in full lycra and cycling shoes at the kitchen island, an empty band across the top for the title. Same style as the Golf and Padel covers.

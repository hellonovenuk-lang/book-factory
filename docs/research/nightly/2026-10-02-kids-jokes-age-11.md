# Proposal: "Jokes for 11 Year Olds" (kids' joke books, a new line under a new pen name)

Nightly research run, 2 October 2026. Angle 3 of the rotation (a new line).

## 1. Decision needed

**Book: a kids' joke book for 11-year-olds, first of an age series, under a new pen name. Reply `go` or `no` (and why).**

## 2. The pitch

- **Title (working):** *Jokes for 11 Year Olds: 600 Jokes, Riddles and Groan-Worthy Puns That Are Actually Funny (Birthday and Christmas Gift for Ages 10-12)*
- **Who buys it:** parents, grandparents and aunties of 9-12-year-olds, as a birthday or Christmas stocking filler. It is the same family-gift buyer as Dave, but for a child, so it needs its own pen name (a clean kids' brand must never sit next to "Oh F*ck, That's Dave").
- **When:** Christmas 2026 (live by about **7 November**), then evergreen: birthdays all year, and a Christmas-cracker spike each December.
- **Why now:** Circana says the 2026 children's market is growing, led by activity and humour books. UK Jokes & Riddles on Amazon has five small-publisher books in its top 20, and three of them are a joke book for one exact age.
- **What is different:** the lists cover ages 5 to 10 and nothing I could see for 11. Older kids call "awesome jokes for an 8 year old" babyish. Ours is aimed at the child who has just started secondary school: the humour is drier and the page is plain, not cartoony. I could not confirm the age-11 gap on Amazon tonight (see section 3), so treat it as a strong lead, not a fact.

What this says:

- **The format wins on Amazon.** One age, one number in the title, "birthday - Christmas gift" in the subtitle. Five small publishers sit in the UK top 20 and four in the US top 20 with it.
- **It is possible for a newcomer.** Mighty Mammoth's two books are from 2023, have only 324-365 reviews, and still sit at UK #8 and #10. The 2019 books have 2,000-4,000 reviews, so a head-on fight at ages 7-9 would be hard.
- **Our length is a selling point, not a handicap.** UK books are 51-75 pages at about £3.50-4.50; US ones run 111-114 pages at $7.75-$9.99. Ours at 110 pages gives roughly 600 jokes.
- **Licensed books (Minecraft, Pokémon) hold #1 and #13**: ignore them, we are not competing for that buyer.
- **Pricing risk:** UK competitors look cheap. Under £7.99 KDP pays only 50%. See section 4.

## 4. The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 inch paperback, black and white, **110 pages** (the most KDP's flat £1.93 / $2.30 printing cost allows).
- **Price and earnings:** UK £7.99 earns about **£2.86** a copy; US $9.99 about **$3.69**. At £6.99 the UK earning drops to about £1.57, so I recommend launching at £7.99 and testing £6.99 only if it does not move. Compare the Dave books at about £4.06.
- **Pen name:** new, kids-only. Placeholder: **Otis Pickle** (publisher imprint "Pickle Jar Press"). Kieran chooses the real one; I have not checked it is free on Amazon. Each pen name is just text on the cover and the KDP form.
- **Contents (about 600 jokes, 6 per page-pair):**
  1. Welcome page: "Warning: may cause eye-rolling" (1 page).
  2. Eight chapters of about 70 jokes each: School and Homework; Animals; Food; Sport (UK football, swimming, the lot); Tech and Gaming (no brand names); Space, Dinosaurs and Science; Groan-Worthy Puns; Riddles and Brain-Benders.
  3. Between chapters, a one-page "Joke Court": the reader scores the jokes (tick boxes, a "groan-o-meter"), made with the existing `activity` blocks.
  4. A "Tell it like a pro" page (timing, the pause, never explain it).
  5. Answers to the riddles at the back; a "make your own" page of write-in lines.
- **Pictures: none inside.** All type, so no picture approvals and no redraw risk. The only picture is the cover.
- **Cover direction:** a big-lettering title with a big "11" in a badge, a loud flat colour, a grinning cartoon mascot (a pickle with a mouth). One Higgsfield picture, about 2 credits; I could not check the balance tonight (the Higgsfield tool is not available in this run). The last known balance is 6 credits. If the picture fails, a type-only cover (the operator's choice, `AGENTS.md` 9a) still works.

## 5. Samples

**Back-cover blurb (draft):**
> You are eleven. You are too old for "Why did the chicken cross the road?" and too young to be taken seriously. This book fixes the first problem. 600 jokes, riddles and puns that make adults groan and friends laugh, sorted by chapter so you always find a good one. Tell them at lunch. Tell them at Christmas. Tell them to your little brother until he leaves the room. Includes a Joke Court so you can score every one.

**Sample jokes (original wording, in the style of the book):**

1. Why was the maths textbook so sad? It had too many problems.
2. What do you call a dinosaur who never gives up? A try-try-try-ceratops.
3. My Wi-Fi went down during the family meeting. Everyone had to talk to each other. It was awful.
4. Why did the football manager bring string to the match? To tie the game.
5. What is a ghost's favourite pudding? Boo-berry crumble.
6. Teacher: "Why is your homework in the dog's mouth?" Me: "You told us to hand it in on time, and he's very fast."
7. What do you call cheese that isn't yours? Nacho cheese.
8. Why did the phone wear glasses? It lost all its contacts.
9. What's a pirate's favourite subject at school? Arrrr-t.
10. I told my little sister a joke about a roof. It went over her head.
11. **Riddle:** What gets wetter the more it dries? A towel.
12. **Riddle:** The more you take, the more you leave behind. What am I? Footsteps.
13. **Joke Court:** "Score this one: I used to hate facial hair, but then it grew on me." Groan-o-meter: [ ] Mild [ ] Loud [ ] Sent to bed.

I wrote these myself; every one is a plain pun or riddle format. Before print, a check pass will remove anything that matches a well-known published joke book too closely, and drop any that are not suitable for an 11-year-old.

## 8. Risks and runners-up

**Risks**
- **Age-11 gap unconfirmed** (Amazon blocked me tonight). First job after `go`: check Amazon by hand for "jokes for 11 year olds" and "12 year olds".
- **Crowded niche with a review moat.** A new book has zero reviews against thousands. Mitigation: it is a series, ages 11 and 12 first, then 6 and 7 later with the same layout.
- **Price and royalty:** competitors sell cheap; we hold £7.99 for the 60% rate.
- **Joke originality:** generic puns are common property, but copied lists are not. Every joke gets a check.
- **KDP rules:** disclose the AI-written text; a kids' book is in the category Amazon is enforcing hardest (see sources). Only two new paperbacks a week, which is fine for one book.
- **Brand:** a new pen name has no audience yet. The Dave audience will not carry over.

**Runners-up**
1. **Murder-mystery advent puzzle book** ("24 days of clues"): US Party games has small-publisher advent mysteries at #2, #5, #7 (26-39 reviews, July-August 2026), so it is hot, but it needs the puzzle page built first and would miss Christmas. Better for 2027, or as a spring murder-mystery.
2. **Large-print word search and sudoku for older adults:** steady volume, but a price fight on generic books and it needs the puzzle page too.

# Proposal: a Dave-style Christmas advent book (angle 2, a new format for the same buyers)

Nightly research run, 5 October 2026. Angle 2 of the rotation (the rotation moved on from angle 1, the three wider ideas of 2 October).

## 1. Decision needed

**Book: *The Hobby Addict's Advent Calendar*, a 24-day Christmas countdown book in the Dave voice, live on Amazon by about 15 November. Reply `go` or `no` (and why).**

## 2. The pitch

- **Title (working):** *The Hobby Addict's Advent Calendar: 24 Days of Quizzes, Rulings and Tasks for Getting Through Christmas With Him*
- **Who buys it:** the same person who buys the Dave books (the wife, the partner, the grown-up kids) for the man in their life who lives for golf, padel, running, cycling or football. It is not tied to one sport, so it works for every Dave buyer.
- **When:** Christmas 2026. An advent book has to be in the buyer's hands before **1 December**, so it must be live by about **15 November**. If missed, it is a one-year-late book, so this is the one idea on the list that is only good if we move fast.
- **Why now:** adult advent *books* (24 days of puzzles, one per day) are the new Christmas format. Small publishers fill US Party Games ranks 5, 7, 8, 11, 14, 20 and 25 with them, plus UK Brain Teasers 16 and 24.
- **What is different:** every advent book I found is a murder mystery or an escape room. None is a comedy. Ours is funny first: each day is a short quiz, a "ruling" or a task about living with someone whose hobby is his whole personality.

What this says:

- **A newcomer can rank.** Most of these have 1 to 40 reviews and still sit in the top 25. The one from last year (223 reviews) shows the format also sells in a second year, with the same title.
- **Prices are $10 to $15**, as high as the Dave books. A 50-page joke advent book sells at $14.99, so £9.99 is not a stretch.
- **There is no funny hobby one.** The only comedy entry is a pun book for general use. Nothing is aimed at the person who lives with a hobby addict.
- **The weak spot:** most are three months old and live already. We would arrive in mid-November, late in the season, and the buyers who search "advent book for adults" will see 20 others first. Our edge has to be the Dave look and the "for him" angle, which Amazon search can find ("gift for golf lover", "gift for husband").

## 4. The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 inch paperback, black and white, about **64 pages**: 24 daily spreads of 2 pages, plus cover pages, a "how to use" page and a final "Christmas Day ruling" (about 80 pages with front and back matter, the same size as Golf and Padel).
- **Price and earnings:** UK £9.99 earns about **£4.06** a copy; US $9.99 about **$3.69** (KDP royalty 60%, minus the flat £1.93 / $2.30 printing cost; the first report's table).
- **Pen name:** the Dave brand (Kieran Smith), so it sits next to Golf, Padel, Runner's and the retirement book.
- **Contents:** each day is one of six repeating formats, all made from `activity` blocks (tick boxes, tables, gauges, checklists), which exist today:
  1. **The Quiz** ("Which of these did he say this week?").
  2. **The Ruling** (an official decision on a household argument).
  3. **The Task** ("Today he must do one thing that is not his hobby").
  4. **The Diagnostic** (a tick-box self-test for him, or for you).
  5. **The Gauge** (a dial to fill in: how long since he mentioned it?).
  6. **The Certificate** (days 12 and 24: a cut-out award).
  Day 24 is "Christmas Day: the Official Ceasefire", a signed treaty.
- **Pictures: none inside.** Only the cover (one picture, about 2 credits). I could not check the Higgsfield balance tonight (the tool is not available in this run); the last known balance is 6 credits.
- **Cover direction:** big lettering, a festive red-and-cream palette with the same Dave typeface, a family at a Christmas table while one man holds a hobby item under the table. The same sort of single family scene as the Golf and Padel covers.

## 5. Samples

**Back-cover blurb (draft):**
> Twenty-four days. One man. One hobby he insists is "not a problem". This advent book gives the whole household a daily task, quiz or official ruling to get you all to Christmas Day in one piece. Open one door a day. Read it out loud at breakfast. Make him tick the boxes. Includes a ceasefire treaty for Christmas Day, ready to sign.

**Sample days (original):**

- **Day 3, The Quiz: "Which of these did he say this week?"** Tick all that apply: [ ] "It's only a few hours." [ ] "I'll be back for lunch." [ ] "It's not an expensive one." [ ] "I'm not obsessed." Scoring: 0 ticks, call a doctor. 1 to 2, normal. 3 to 4, see Day 9.
- **Day 7, The Ruling: "The Christmas Day walk."** It has been put to the household that Christmas morning could include a short walk. The household notes that the walk will be 11 miles and called a "recovery session". Ruling: the walk is 20 minutes, round the block, in a coat.
- **Day 11, The Task:** "Ask him one question about anything that is not his hobby. Wait for the answer. Do not let him turn it into the hobby. Time taken before he does: ____ minutes."
- **Day 17, The Gauge:** "Minutes since he last mentioned it" (fill the dial: Calm, Warning, Gift Opened, Mentions It Anyway).
- **Day 24, The Treaty:** "On this day the hobby will be paused from the first present until the last pudding. Signed: ______ (him) ______ (everyone else)."

## 6. What Book Factory needs

- **Nothing to build first.** Every page type above is an `activity` page; no new page type, no grid puzzle, no answer key.
- It still needs the usual path: `/write-book` with a concept, voice (the Dave voice is reusable with `--series-from` the Golf book), manuscript, visual lock, cover and release. The retirement book is already in that queue; this would be the second book at once.
- A hobby-neutral book needs a hobby-neutral main character. Dave (or "he") must not be a golfer. It reads best as a general "Dave" who rotates hobbies across the days.

## 8. Risks and runners-up

- **Risk, timing:** miss 15 November and the best weeks are gone. The book then waits until October 2027 (it could be re-used with the same title).
- **Risk, crowd:** there are many murder-mystery and escape-room advent books. We are the only comedy one I found, but ours is not a puzzle book, so a puzzle fan may be disappointed. The blurb must say "funny, not a mystery".
- **Risk, thin evidence:** I have list ranks, not sales. A cheap test is only one cover picture (2 credits) and about 3 sittings.
- **Runner-up 1:** *The Golf Addict's Puzzle Book* (still "awaiting Kieran" in the index, needs a puzzle page built first, so not before Christmas).

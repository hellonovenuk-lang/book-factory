# Proposal: a Dave-style "Would You Rather" book for couples (angle 2, a new format for the same buyers)

Nightly research run, 12 October 2026. Angle 2 of the rotation (the last run was angle 1, the cyclist book).

## 1. Decision needed

**Book: *Would You Rather? The Hobby Addict's Household Edition*, about 270 funny two-way choices for couples whose weekends belong to someone else's hobby. Reply `go` or `no` (and why).**

## 2. The pitch

- **Title (working):** *Would You Rather? The Hobby Addict's Household Edition: 270 Impossible Choices for Couples Whose Weekends Belong to a Golf Course, a Padel Court or a Bike*
- **Who buys it:** the same people who buy the Dave books (the wife or partner, grown-up kids), for the hobby-obsessed man, and as a "do it together" gift for both of them. It reads as a game, not a lecture, so it also works as a Christmas-Day, car-journey and dinner-table book.
- **When:** Christmas 2026 (live by about 13-20 November), then Valentine's Day and Father's Day.
- **Why now:** "Would you rather?" books are all over the UK Trivia and Quiz lists (ranks 1, 3 and 6 are would-you-rather titles, and an independent kids' one has 6,599 reviews). Almost all are for children or "naughty" couples. A funny, clean, household-life one for hobby-obsessed men and their partners is not there.
- **Different from Amazon:** each choice is a Dave-world dilemma, not a random one: "Would you rather hear the full story of the 7th hole, or be told the 7th hole 'doesn't need to come up'?" It also sits next to our other books and sells them.

## 4. The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 in, black and white, about 100 pages. List price £8.99 UK / $9.99 US. KDP's printing cost is £1.93 / $2.30 up to 110 pages (first report, `docs/research/2026-09-30-new-book-types.md`, which also explains the royalty rule). That earns about **£3.46 a copy in the UK and $3.69 in the US**. (Web guides disagree on KDP's exact printing figures; check KDP's own calculator at upload.) At £9.99 it would earn about £4.06, but the book's price sits next to £5-£10 quiz books, so £8.99 is the better first price.
- **Pen name:** the Dave brand, same big-lettering cover as Golf and Padel.
- **Contents (about 270 choices, roughly three a page):**
  1. How to play (rules: both must answer, no appeals, "the 7th hole is not a valid reason")
  2. Round 1: Weekend Mornings (the 6am alarm, the "quick" ride, the long breakfast he wasn't at)
  3. Round 2: The Money (equipment, memberships, "it was about £800")
  4. Round 3: Holidays and Family Occasions (a trip that is "near a course", a wedding with a fixture clash)
  5. Round 4: Talking (stories you have heard, the stat nobody asked for)
  6. Round 5: The Garage, The Hall and The Washing Line (kit, storage, drying)
  7. Round 6: The Big Ones (final tie-breakers, "would you rather he retired or took up another hobby?")
  8. Scorecard: a page where each of you tallies which of you chose what, with a "Final Ruling" certificate to cut out
  9. Answer key: "What your answers say about your household" (a short verdict page, funny not rude)
- **Pictures:** none inside. One cover picture (about 2 credits). I could not read the Higgsfield balance tonight (the connector is not available in this scheduled run); last known balance was 6 credits (the Retirement cover will draw some).
- **Cover direction:** the couple at the dinner table, her holding a card, him with a putter or racket at the ready, an empty band across the top for the title; the same style as the other covers.

## 5. Samples (original)

**Back cover:** "You love each other. You also love, in his case, golf, padel, running, cycling and being right about the 7th. This book puts you both on the spot. Pick one. No explaining. Dave is not allowed to tell the story of how he got out of that bunker."

**Ten sample choices:**
1. Would you rather hear the full account of his round, hole by hole, or be told the 7th "went fine" and nothing else?
2. Would you rather he got up at 5.30am and woke the house, or got home at 7pm and said "I'm back" at the volume of a man announcing a victory?
3. Would you rather the bike lived in the hall, or lived in the bedroom, where it is "safer"?
4. Would you rather he gave you the real price of the new racket, or the price he told himself?
5. Would you rather spend a Saturday at a match you don't care about, or a Saturday at a party he doesn't care about, where he will talk about the match?
6. Would you rather he asked how your day was and listened for four seconds, or told you about his for forty minutes?
7. Would you rather his kit dried on the radiator, or on the banister, or in a different room that is "just for now"?
8. Would you rather he had one new hobby for a year, or three brief obsessions in a month?
9. Would you rather he tracked every step on an app and told you the total, or stopped tracking and told you he "just knows"?
10. Would you rather he took the family on a holiday near a course, or took himself on a holiday near a course and called it "a break for you"?

**Sample "Final Ruling" page:** "You answered A to 9 of 10. The household has a ruling: A is correct. He may now stop talking about it."

## 6. What Book Factory needs

- **Can it make this today?** Probably yes, with no build work. The `activity` page type (`docs/RENDERING.md`) can set a column of numbered choices with tick boxes and a scorecard as real type. Three choices to a page is the one thing to fit-test early (`plan --from-manuscript` reports pages that overflow). If it does not fit well, a small layout tweak is a single sitting at most; I have not run a trial, so treat this as likely rather than certain.
- Copy to write: about 270 short choices plus rules, scorecard, verdict pages and cover copy (the `/write-book` skill does all of this).
- Picture budget: set to `chapter_openers` by default; this book has no page pictures, so only the cover.

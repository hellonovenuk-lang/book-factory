# The Hyrox Addict's Guide to Talking About Anything Else (Dave series)

## Decision needed

**Book: *The Hyrox Addict's Guide to Talking About Anything Else*. Reply `go` or `no` (and why).**

## The pitch

- **Title:** *The Hyrox Addict's Guide to Talking About Anything Else: An Official Handbook for the Person Who Races, and the Household That Watches*
- **Who buys:** the partner, family, gym mates and colleagues of someone who does Hyrox (a fitness race: eight 1 km runs, each followed by a gym station such as sled push or wall balls). It works for him or her, which the other Dave books do not.
- **When:** Christmas (live by about 7 November, realistically mid-November), then New Year (the fitness spike) and birthdays.
- **Why now:** Hyrox is growing very fast and its fans talk about nothing else, which is the exact Dave joke. It is #2 in `docs/BOOK-IDEAS.md` and needs no build work.
- **Different from Amazon:** every Hyrox book I could find is a training manual or workout log. I found no humour book, and none written for the people who live with a racer.

## The book plan

- **Format:** 6 x 9 in, black and white, about 80 pages, £9.99 / $9.99, earning about £4.06 / $3.69 a copy (royalty table in `docs/research/2026-09-30-new-book-types.md`). Dave brand, big-lettering cover like Golf and Padel.
- **Contents:**
  1. Official notice and the Terms of Racing (a mock contract)
  2. Assessment: "Are you an athlete or a person who has a race date?" (tick-list)
  3. The Splits Register: what he knows to the second (wall-ball split) against what he doesn't (the child's shoe size)
  4. The Everything-Is-a-Station Survey: sled push in the car park, farmer's carry with the shopping
  5. The "Holiday" Converter: the race abroad that is called a holiday
  6. Pacing Guide for Family Events (do not go out too fast at the wedding)
  7. Fuel Rules: what is allowed on the table at Sunday lunch
  8. Recovery Day Rota: the day nobody may speak to him
  9. The Entry Fee Ledger: ballot, hotel, trainers, "the vest"
  10. A Gauge: how close to a conversation about anything else
  11. Cut-out Certificate: Permission to Discuss the Weather
  12. The signed Household Treaty
- **Pictures:** one cover picture only (about 2 credits). I could not read the Higgsfield balance (not connected in this run). Last known: 6 credits, minus the Retirement cover when it is drawn. No interior pictures.
- **Cover direction:** the family at the Sunday table, Dave in a race vest holding a roast chicken like a wall ball, an empty band across the top for the title.
- **Trademark note:** HYROX is a registered brand. Use the word only as plain descriptive text in the subtitle and keywords, with no logo or race branding, and re-read KDP's trademark help page before upload. If that feels too risky, the safe title is *The Fitness Racer's Guide to Talking About Anything Else*, at the cost of search appeal.

## Samples (original)

**Back cover:** "He knows his wall-ball split to the second. He does not know your mother's name. This handbook is for the people who stood at the finish line holding the coats."

**Sample lines:**
- "Wall-ball split, as recalled at dinner: 4:12. Child's shoe size, as recalled at dinner: a number."
- "Not every car park is a training venue. Please advise him of this before he buys the sled."
- "The race abroad is not a holiday. A holiday contains a day on which nobody is timed."
- "Recovery day rule 1: he is resting. He may rest in the kitchen. He may not rest at length about the kitchen."

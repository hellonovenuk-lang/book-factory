# Audience - The Runner’s Guide to Normal Conversation

## Primary buyer

A partner, friend, family member or office colleague who knows the recipient’s running monologues well enough to recognise the premise immediately.

## Primary recipient

A recreational runner who can laugh at their own habits, especially race planning, watch checking and turning small talk into a training debrief.

## Buying occasions

Christmas and office Secret Santa, birthdays, race completion and a playful present from a partner or friend.

## Comparable titles

| Title | What it does well | What we do differently |
| ----- | ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| Illustrated humour and hobby gift books | Easy to recognise as a present | Specific social observations and an 80-page mock rehabilitation programme. |

## Sensitivities

What would make this land badly for the recipient?

Affectionate recognition. Avoid implying that running itself is a problem or that the manual gives real medical treatment. The recipient should be able to open it in front of family or colleagues without feeling mocked for their body, fitness or health.

# Concept - The Runner’s Guide to Normal Conversation

> Locked at concept lock. Later stages may not renegotiate what is written here.

## Premise

The fictional Normal Conversation Rehabilitation Service has issued a manual to people whose running has escaped into every conversation. The preserved 80-page interior is the authority for its jokes, characters and sequence.

## Structure of the book

How does the reader move through it? (Programme? Stages? Chapters as escalating
severity? A diagnosis-to-cure arc?)

The reader progresses from referral and diagnosis through identifying triggers, basic conversation practice, life outside the training plan, equipment habits, advanced social exposure and relapse prevention. Tests and exercises culminate in a certificate of conditional discharge.

## Running jokes

Bits that reappear. Note where each one pays off.

Alex turns ordinary questions into route commentary, monitors the watch in inappropriate settings, justifies shoe purchases, adapts holidays around running, and has to learn to hear Sam’s and Pat’s answers. The certificate qualifies the apparent cure.

## What this book is NOT

Neither an anti-running polemic nor a real clinical guide. No affiliation with any race, running service, sportswear company or watch manufacturer.

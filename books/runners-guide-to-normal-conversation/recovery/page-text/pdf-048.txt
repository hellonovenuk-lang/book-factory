Buying-decision check
Ask:

1. Would I want this if I stopped running for one
month?

2. Have I described it as an investment?

3. Does the claimed saving involve seconds?

4. Am I comparing £12 of normal spending with £180
of running spending?

5. Did a person in a vest review it online?




Three yes answers require a twenty-four-hour pause.

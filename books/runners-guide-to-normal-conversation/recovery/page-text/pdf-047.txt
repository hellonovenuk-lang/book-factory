Shopping




Every purchase need not support running.

  When choosing a car, boot capacity may be discussed
without arranging imaginary muddy shoes inside it.

  When choosing a home, distance to a park may matter. It
must not erase the roof, boiler or feelings of the other
buyer.

  When choosing a kettle, there is no race-day model.

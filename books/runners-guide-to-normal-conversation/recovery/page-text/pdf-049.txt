Family events
Family events are fixed points, not obstacles placed by
people who failed to consult your plan.

  If a long run and a birthday lunch occupy the same
morning, available solutions include running earlier,
running shorter, moving the run or missing it. ‘Arrive during
pudding in compression tights’ has been removed from the
approved list.

  Never ask an entire family to move Christmas lunch
because you need four hours between food and running.
Christmas has seniority.

 Dr Hughes accepts that turkey can be dry. This does not
make it performance nutrition.

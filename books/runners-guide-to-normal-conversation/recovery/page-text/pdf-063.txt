The faster runner




Exposure to a faster recreational runner may produce
defensive context.

  Common symptoms include mentioning age categories,
elevation, recent illness, accumulated fatigue, a missing
pacer and the fact that you were ‘never really racing it’.

  Listen to the result.

  Congratulate the runner.

  Allow the number to remain smaller than yours.

  If asked about your own PB, answer. If not asked, the
record remains safe where it is.

 Dr Hughes notes that a personal best survives periods in
which it is not spoken aloud.

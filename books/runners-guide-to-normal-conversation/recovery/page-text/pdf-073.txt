             RELAPSE PREVENTION AND CONDITIONAL DISCHARGE




The ‘just an easy one’
intervention




The phrase appears modest. It contains distance, pace and a
pre-emptive defence against an accusation nobody made.

  When you feel it approaching, choose one alternative:

  ‘I went for a run.’

  ‘I was out for a bit.’

  ‘Yes, it was nice.’

  Silence.

  If the distance matters, the other person will ask.

  If they do not ask, place it carefully back inside yourself.

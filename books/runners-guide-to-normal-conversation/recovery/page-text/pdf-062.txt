When somebody else runs
Another person may run.

  This can occur without reducing the significance of your
own running. You are not required to establish who runs
farther, earlier, faster or with less cushioning.

     When they share a result, say ‘Well done.’

  Do not immediately ask whether the course was short. Do
not inspect their activity for pauses. Do not explain that
your own result happened during a heavier training week.

 The words ‘Well done’ remain accurate even when their
watch model is inferior.

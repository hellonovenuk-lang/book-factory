Restaurants
A menu is not a fuelling plan with prices.

  Choose something because you would like to eat it. Avoid
interviewing the waiter about the dry weight of the rice. A
meal can contain carbohydrates without becoming carb
loading; the distinction is important to everybody except
your digestive system.

  Complete one meal without saying:

  ‘I’ve earned this.’

  ‘I need the salt.’

  ‘Good recovery food.’

  ‘This might sit a bit heavy tomorrow.’

  ‘I’m not normally this bad.’

  You are eating chips, Alex. No governing body has opened
an inquiry.

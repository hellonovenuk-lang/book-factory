Injury conversation




An injury is painful, frustrating and capable of making
running occupy even more conversation than running did.

   You may tell close people how you feel. You may ask for
support. The Service distinguishes this from issuing daily
tissue updates to colleagues.

   When resting, do not introduce yourself as ‘currently
injured’. Your name remains available.

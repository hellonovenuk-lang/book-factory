Stage Three assessment
1. For the first week, any account of a run must end
after two sentences.

2. Begin with the words ‘Thank you.’ Stop there.

3. After one statement about yourself, ask a relevant
question about them. Listen to the answer without
scanning it for entry points.

4. Lower the wrist.

5. Keep the phone out of your hand.

6. Let the listener choose whether to ask more.




An expression of warmth is a response, not informed consent for the route
plan.

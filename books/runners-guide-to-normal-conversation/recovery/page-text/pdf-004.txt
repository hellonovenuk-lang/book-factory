    Conditions of
     treatment
  The Normal Conversation Rehabilitation Service offers
 social guidance, not medical or training advice. Continue
 running if running makes you happy. Continue resting if a
 qualified professional tells you to rest. The Service has no
                  opinion on your cadence.

Any resemblance to a runner you know is intentional in the
general sense and accidental in every legally useful sense.

Brand names appear only where ordinary runners use them
in ordinary speech. No brand has approved the programme.
   Several would prefer you to remain exactly as you are.

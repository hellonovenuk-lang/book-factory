The ordinary question test




                Pat only asked about the weekend.
A normal person hears: “Did you have a nice weekend?”
They say: “Yes, thanks. Quiet one. How about you?”

  You hear the same question and begin at 06:15 on
Sunday. You explain the porridge, the weather app, the
missing sock, the first three kilometres, the decision not to
chase pace, the pace you accidentally chased, the headwind
near the retail park and the reason the final average was
not representative.

  The question has not been answered. It has been outlived.

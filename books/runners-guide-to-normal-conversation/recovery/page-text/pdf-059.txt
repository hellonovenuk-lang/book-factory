Race fees
Race entries occupy a protected part of the budget where
normal arithmetic cannot reach them.

  The fee pays for a closed road, a measured route, medical
support, a medal, timing and the privilege of doing publicly
what you often do free. This can be worthwhile. The
problem begins when entering becomes the treatment for
feeling uncertain about the races already entered.

  List every confirmed event.

  Beside each, record travel, accommodation, food and the
conversation in which Sam first heard about it.

  If the final column says ‘confirmation email’, apologise
before entering another.

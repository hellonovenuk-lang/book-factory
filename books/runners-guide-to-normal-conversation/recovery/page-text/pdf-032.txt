The return question
Conversational recovery depends on returning attention to
the other person.

   After one statement about yourself, ask a relevant
question about them. Listen to the answer without scanning
it for entry points.

     Sam: We went to York at the weekend. You: Was it good?

     The following responses fail the exercise:

     ‘I ran York once.’

     ‘York is flatter than people think.’

     ‘Did you go near the racecourse?’

     ‘I’ve always said it would be a good marathon city.’

     ‘How many steps did you do?’

  The question belongs to Sam. Allow York to remain
unmeasured.

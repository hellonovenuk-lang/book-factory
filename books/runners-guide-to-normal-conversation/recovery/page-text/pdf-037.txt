A controlled disclosure
You may share running news when it matters to you.
Recovery does not require secrecy; it requires proportion.

  Use this structure:

  1. State the news.

  2. State why you care.

  3. Stop.

  4. Let the listener choose whether to ask more.

  Example:

   ‘I got a place in the London Marathon. I’ve wanted to do
it for years, so I’m delighted.’

  This is complete. Training volume, charity targets, hotel
availability and the ballot system remain available if
requested.

  An expression of warmth is a response, not informed
consent for the route plan.

‘Fine, thanks’
The phrase ‘Fine, thanks’ contains two words and one
complete answer.

You may find this suspiciously brief. Normal conversation
relies on such compression. People often exchange broad
summaries before deciding whether detail is required.

PAT                                         YO U

  How are you?                                 Fine, thanks. How are
                                               you?




Pause. Pat may now answer. Resist the impulse to use the pause for a calf
update. If Pat asks whether you have been running, you may say yes. This
is not permission to locate the activity.

Case file: Alex
Alex is a recreational runner of ordinary talent and
exceptional administrative commitment.

  On referral, Alex owned two formal outfits and eleven
garments described as layers. Their watch had recorded a
stressful afternoon during their own birthday meal. They
had entered three races, complained about having too many
races and entered a fourth while explaining the problem to
Sam.

  Asked why running came up so often, Alex said it did not.
Alex then supplied examples from five recent conversations
to prove this, all of which were about running.

  Dr Hughes recorded the patient as cooperative, articulate
and likely to convert treatment into content.

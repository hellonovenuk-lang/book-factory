Bad-weather group conversation




During severe weather, runners gather around one central
question: whether going out proves admirable commitment
or poor judgement.

     You are not required to answer publicly.

  If official advice says avoid unnecessary travel, do not
post a windswept photograph with the words ‘No excuses’.
The fire service has other uses for its afternoon.

  A treadmill session does not require an apology. A rest
day does not require quotation marks.

  Weather cannot see your activity and will not feel
defeated.

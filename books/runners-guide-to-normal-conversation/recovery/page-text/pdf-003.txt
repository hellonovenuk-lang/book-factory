  This manual
has been issued
because people
  close to you
 have noticed a
    pattern.
The pattern may involve pace, distance, shoes, fuelling, race
 entries, recovery scores, weather conditions, a calf that is
“basically fine” or a run described as easy despite requiring
                   two gels and a lift home.

  Receipt of this manual does not confirm that you have a
 problem. Your immediate attempt to compare the problem
     with last Tuesday’s tempo session probably does.

Keep the manual somewhere visible. Do not place it beneath
the foam roller, inside the drawer of race numbers or beside
    the unopened cookbooks purchased when you briefly
      became interested in carbohydrate periodisation.

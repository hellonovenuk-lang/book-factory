Other people’s weekends
Ask somebody what they did at the weekend.

  While they answer, keep attention on their account. Do
not rank the activities by training benefit.

  If they walked, it was a walk. If they cycled, it was a
cycle. If they did nothing, they did not have a recovery day
unless they choose that term.

  At the end, ask one follow-up question drawn from what
they actually said.

  Do not reward yourself by explaining your Sunday run.
The exercise is complete when the conversation ends and
your route remains unknown.

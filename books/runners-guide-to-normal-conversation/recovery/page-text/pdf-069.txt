Race week
Race week narrows the conversational field.

You may become interested in forecasts that do not yet
exist, carbohydrate sources you do not normally eat and the
exact health of your throat. Every person who coughs
becomes an active threat.

Race-week limits

    One weather update per day.

    One shoe decision, reversible only for actual
    damage.

    No public throat reports.

    No asking colleagues whether the office feels
    warm.

    No announcing the final toilet visit.

    No use of ‘the hay is in the barn’ unless you own
    either.




Sam may withdraw conversational services forty-eight hours before the
start.

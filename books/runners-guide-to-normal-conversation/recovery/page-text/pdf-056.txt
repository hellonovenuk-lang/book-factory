The watch upgrade
Your current watch measures time, distance, heart rate,
elevation, sleep, recovery and several things you cannot
define without opening the app.

     The new watch measures all of these more brightly.

   Before upgrading, write down the decision the new
metric will change. ‘It will be useful to know’ is not a
decision. ‘Battery life’ is accepted only if your present watch
is regularly dying rather than merely reaching 38 per cent.

  Alex justified an upgrade using maps, then continued
running the same loop from home.

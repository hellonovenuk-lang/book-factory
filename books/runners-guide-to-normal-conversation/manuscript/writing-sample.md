## Sample 1 - a typical internal passage

### Reason for referral

It now occupies breakfast, weekends, online activity, family logistics and the first twelve minutes of any conversation with a person careless enough to ask how you are. Friends know which toenail is causing concern. Colleagues know your target race. Your partner can distinguish a recovery run from an easy run but wishes they could not.

No single behaviour caused the referral. The Service acts when the behaviours form a training block.

Common referral sources include a colleague trapped beside the kettle, a partner who has moved a holiday twice, and a family group chat containing more route screenshots than photographs of family members.

## Sample 2 - a structured bit (checklist, test, table copy)

### Initial severity assessment

Award one point for each true statement:

1. Your watch has vibrated during a funeral, wedding ceremony or disciplinary meeting.
2. You own more running shoes than shoes suitable for the rest of your life.
3. A partner has said, “I wasn’t asking about the run.”
4. You have described rain as “actually quite refreshing” while visibly shivering.
5. You have uploaded an activity before removing your wet clothes.
6. You have used a minor injury to begin a forty-minute monologue about training load.
7. You know a colleague’s 10 km PB but not the name of their partner.
8. You have booked a holiday after checking whether the hotel is near a flat route.
9. You have called 15 km “nothing silly”.
10. You are reading these statements while disagreeing with the scoring methodology.

Record the score before proceeding.

*Source: interior PDF page 10, printed page 4; line breaks regularised from the PDF extraction.*

# Audience - The Golf Addict's Guide to Family Reintegration

## Primary buyer

A partner, grown-up child or friend who loves a golf addict and has lost weekends, dinners and conversations to the game.

## Primary recipient

An adult golfer, most often a man aged 40 to 70, who plays several times a week, owns more golf equipment than garden tools and can describe every round in detail.

## Buying occasions

Father's Day, birthdays, Christmas and Secret Santa, plus retirement (when the golf is about to get worse) and joining a new club.

## Comparable titles

| Title | What it does well | What we do differently |
| ----- | ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| General golf joke books | Quick laughs about bad shots | Ours is about the family at home, which is who buys it |
| "Rules of golf" parody books | Play the rules straight for comedy | Ours runs as a re-entry programme with exercises and a certificate |
| Hobby-obsession gift books (e.g. our own Runner's Guide) | Recognition of one habit | Ours applies the same straight-faced programme to golf |

## Sensitivities

What would make this land badly for the recipient?

Anything that reads as a real complaint about his marriage or parenting, or suggests the family would be happier without him. The joke must be that they miss him and want him back for more than a few hours at a time.

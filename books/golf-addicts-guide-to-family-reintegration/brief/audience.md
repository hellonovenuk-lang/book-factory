# Audience - The Golf Addict's Guide to Family Reintegration

## Primary buyer

A UK partner, grown-up son or daughter, or mate who loves a golf addict and has lost Saturdays, Sunday dinners and whole conversations to the game.

## Primary recipient

A UK golfer, usually a bloke aged 40 to 70, who plays several times a week, owns more golf gear than garden tools, can describe every round in detail and has opinions about the club committee.

## Buying occasions

Father's Day, birthdays, Christmas and Secret Santa, plus retirement (when the golf is about to get much worse) and joining a new club.

## Comparable titles

| Title | What it does well | What we do differently |
| ----- | ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| General golf joke books | Quick laughs about bad shots | Ours is about the family at home, who are the ones buying it |
| "Rules of golf" parody books | Play the rules straight for comedy | Ours runs as an official re-entry programme with exercises and a certificate |
| Hobby-obsession gift books (e.g. our own Runner's Guide) | Recognition of one habit | Ours is cheekier and more British, for adults |

## Sensitivities

What would make this land badly for the recipient?

Anything that reads as a real complaint about his marriage or parenting, or suggests the family would be better off without him. The joke is that they miss the daft sod and want him back for more than a few hours at a time. The swearing must feel like family banter, never like an attack, and should be fine to read aloud at Christmas to adults.

# Outline - The Golf Addict's Guide to Family Reintegration

> One line per chapter saying what job the chapter does. Detail lives in the
> manuscript; this is the shape of the book.

## Front matter

- Title page, notice of issue, conditions of the programme, contents (about 5 pages).

## Chapters

### Stage One - Arrival
**Job:** Set up the Programme, Sue as case sponsor, the family, and how far gone he is.
**Best joke:** The initial assessment's top score: "For fuck's sake, Dave."
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Two - Communication
**Job:** Answering a question, the putt on the 7th, talking and listening without golf.
**Best joke:** The emergency putt card, kept next to the 2019 scorecard.
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Three - The Household
**Job:** A tour of the house he pays for: garage, lawn, jobs, swinging household objects.
**Best joke:** The eleven putters, and the flag in the lawn from Keith.
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Four - Time
**Job:** Golf time against family time: the quick nine, the calendar, the forecast.
**Best joke:** The golf time conversion chart ("Quick nine" = five hours).
**Page budget:** 8

### Stage Five - Family Occasions
**Job:** Turning up to birthdays, anniversaries, school events and weddings, in full.
**Best joke:** Becky's wedding on medal day ("Can't they move it?").
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Six - Holidays
**Job:** Choosing and surviving a family holiday not picked for the courses.
**Best joke:** Losing to his six-year-old grandson at crazy golf in Cornwall.
**Page budget:** 8

### Stage Seven - Relapse Prevention
**Job:** Triggers, warning signs, Keith, and a plan for the first sunny Saturday in April.
**Best joke:** The £449 driver that was "a present for Keith".
**Page budget:** 10

### Stage Eight - Graduation
**Job:** Final assessment, the family finishing the putt story, and the certificate.
**Best joke:** The whole family saying "lipped out" together.
**Page budget:** 9

## Back matter

- Certificate of Reintegration (full page) and a short closing note (about 3 pages, with the certificate counted in Stage Eight).

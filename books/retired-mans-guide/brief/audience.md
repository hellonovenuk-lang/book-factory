# Audience - The Newly Retired Man's Guide to Being Home All Day

## Primary buyer

A UK wife in her sixties who has had the house to herself on weekdays for decades and now shares it with a husband who wants to help. Close behind: grown-up children buying for their dad, and work colleagues clubbing together for a leaving present.

## Primary recipient

A UK man in his mid-sixties who has just retired after a long career, often in management or a job with systems and targets, fit and full of energy, now home all day and bringing his working habits with him into the kitchen, the shed and the supermarket.

## Buying occasions

The leaving do and the retirement card, his first birthday, Christmas and Father's Day after he stops work, and a retirement party. Leaving gifts happen all year, so the book is not tied to Christmas.

## Sensitivities

What would make this land badly for the recipient?

Anything that reads as "you are old now": jokes about bodies, memory, health, money running out or death are out, as fixed at intake. So is anything that makes him look useless or unwanted: the joke is that he is capable and full of energy and has pointed it at the cupboards. It must be safe to read aloud at a leaving do in front of the managing director and at Christmas in front of the grandchildren, so swearing stays rare and mild, and Sue is always sharp and in charge, never a nag.

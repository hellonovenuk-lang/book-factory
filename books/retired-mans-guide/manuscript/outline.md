# Outline - The Newly Retired Man's Guide to Being Home All Day

> One line per chapter saying what job the chapter does. Detail lives in the
> manuscript; this is the shape of the book.

## Front matter

- Title page, letter of appointment, terms and conditions, contents.

## Chapters

### Stage One: Welcome to the Household
**Job:** Set up the frame (he has joined the Household as a new starter), introduce Sue, Ellie and Biscuit on the org chart, and measure how eager his start has been.
**Best joke:** Biscuit the dog ranks above him on the organisation chart.
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Two: Your First Week
**Job:** The 6:15 alarm, a week in the life, what work used to give him and the agenda nobody asked for.
**Best joke:** He sent Sue a calendar invitation for the big shop.
**Page budget:** 8

### Stage Three: The Kitchen
**Job:** The cupboard reorganisation, the Kitchen Boundary Agreement, the Dishwasher Loading Review and what "helping" means.
**Best joke:** There are now two dishwasher methods. Only one is correct, and it has never been his.
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Four: Projects
**Job:** The Project Register: every "quick job" with its estimate and its actual time, the shed, the loft and the tip.
**Best joke:** The man at the tip now waves.
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Five: Out and About
**Job:** The big shop, the garden centre, the bins and the neighbours.
**Best joke:** The bin collection spreadsheet, now shared with the street.
**Page budget:** 8

### Stage Six: Family Duties
**Job:** Ellie, the nursery run he calls "the logistics", and phone calls to the grown-up children about the loft.
**Best joke:** Ellie, three, runs his diary better than his old PA did.
**Page budget:** 8

### Stage Seven: Keeping Busy
**Job:** "Busier than ever", the weather briefing, hobbies tried and dropped, and finding something of his own.
**Best joke:** "Busier than ever", and then the bins.
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Eight: Your Probation Review
**Job:** A diagnostic of his new routines, Sue's feedback and a week-by-week adjustment plan.
**Best joke:** He slept until twenty to eight and reported it as a personal best.
**Page budget:** 9

### Stage Nine: The Retirement Treaty
**Job:** Final review, a Tuesday left alone, the signed Treaty and the certificate.
**Best joke:** Sue asks "what are we doing today?" first, and he has no answer, and enjoys it.
**Page budget:** 8

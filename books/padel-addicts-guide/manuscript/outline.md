# Outline - The Padel Addict's Guide to Talking About Anything Else

> One line per chapter saying what job the chapter does. Detail lives in the
> manuscript; this is the shape of the book.

## Front matter

- Title page, notice of engagement, terms of engagement, contents.

## Chapters

### Phase One: Scoping
**Job:** Set up the engagement, introduce the stakeholders and measure how far gone Josh is.
**Best joke:** He asked the bank's mortgage adviser whether she played.
**Page budget:** 9

### Phase Two: Current State
**Job:** A day in the life of Josh, what padel has pushed out, and the "fastest-growing sport" tally.
**Best joke:** The smartwatch knows more about his Tuesday doubles than his GP knows about him.
**Page budget:** 8

### Phase Three: Talking About Anything Else
**Job:** The core module: answering the question asked, other topics, a daily padel-mention budget and listening.
**Best joke:** The emergency bandeja card.
**Page budget:** 10

### Phase Four: The Home Front
**Job:** Bath time, the 6am group chat, the car boot and what Mia thinks Daddy does.
**Best joke:** Mia thinks "padel" is a swear word because of how Mummy says it.
**Page budget:** 8

### Phase Five: The Office
**Job:** Stand-ups, the team social vote, LinkedIn, the client lunch and Priya's tennis.
**Best joke:** Three quarters running the team social went to bowling.
**Page budget:** 8

### Phase Six: Recruitment Ban
**Job:** Leave his dad, his father-in-law, the neighbours and the boiler man alone.
**Best joke:** Graham, 71, with a new hip.
**Page budget:** 6

### Phase Seven: Holidays and Occasions
**Job:** The "family" trip to Spain, what to wear to a party, presents, and a wedding on tournament day.
**Best joke:** The kids' club is right next to the courts.
**Page budget:** 8

### Phase Eight: Relapse Risk
**Job:** The risk register, warning signs, the new court opening and muting the group chat.
**Best joke:** The risk register rates "the club opens a fourth court" as almost certain and catastrophic.
**Page budget:** 7

### Phase Nine: Sign-off
**Job:** Final review, stakeholder feedback, the dinner party test, Priya's tennis rematch and the certificate.
**Best joke:** Mia says "fastest-growing sport in the world" before he can.
**Page budget:** 8

# Audience - The Padel Addict's Guide to Talking About Anything Else

## Primary buyer

A UK partner, usually a wife or girlfriend in her late twenties or thirties with small children and a job, who has lost weekends, dinners and whole conversations to padel. Close behind: a colleague buying for the office Secret Santa, a sibling, a parent or an old friend.

## Primary recipient

A UK professional in his late twenties or early thirties, often in an office job, who took up padel recently and now plays several times a week, owns more rackets than he needs, runs the group chat and talks about it to everyone, at home and at work.

## Buying occasions

Birthdays, Christmas, Father's Day and the office Secret Santa, plus joining a new club, a padel holiday booking and a new baby on the way (when court time is about to get much harder).

## Sensitivities

What would make this land badly for the recipient?

Anything that reads as a real complaint about his marriage or his parenting, or as mockery of him being a young dad. The joke is that everyone likes Josh and would like him more if he talked about something else now and then. It has to be safe to open in front of colleagues at work and in front of the in-laws at Christmas, so any swearing stays rare and mild, and his job is teased, never his competence.
